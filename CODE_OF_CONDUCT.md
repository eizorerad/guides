# Code of conduct

Project discussions, issues, reviews and other project-related interactions
should be respectful and constructive. Discuss scientific and technical ideas
on their merits, welcome questions and acknowledge mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and publication of
someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to Leonid Klarov at <leonid.klarov@gmail.com>.
Include enough context to understand the incident without posting private
details in a public issue. Reports will be handled discreetly; information
should be shared only as needed to address the concern.

The maintainer may request corrections, remove inappropriate contributions
or restrict participation in project-managed spaces. Responses should be
proportionate to the conduct and consider the affected participants.
